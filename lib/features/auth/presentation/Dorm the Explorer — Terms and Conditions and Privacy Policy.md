# Part 1: Terms and Conditions

## 1. Acceptance of Terms

Welcome to Dorm the Explorer ("the App", "we", "us"). By creating an account or using the App, you agree to these Terms and Conditions and to our Privacy Policy below. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 2. About the App

Dorm the Explorer is a mobile platform that helps students and young professionals find dormitories and boarding houses in Batangas City, and helps landlords list and manage their properties. We provide a place to publish and discover listings. We are not a landlord, agent, or broker, and we are not a party to any rental agreement between a seeker and a landlord.

## 3. Accounts and Eligibility

- You must provide accurate information when you register, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
- You choose one role when you register: Seeker or Landlord.
- You must be at least 18 years old, or have the consent of a parent or guardian, to use the App.
- Landlords must be the owner of the property they list, or be authorized by the owner to list it.
- You are responsible for keeping your password secure and for all activity under your account. Tell us right away if you think your account has been used without your permission.

## 4. Responsibilities of Seekers

As a Seeker, you agree to:

- Use the App only to look for housing and to contact landlords in good faith.
- Post reviews and ratings that are honest and based on your own experience.
- Verify a listing yourself before paying anything. Visit the dorm, meet the landlord, and read any contract carefully.

## 5. Responsibilities of Landlords

As a Landlord, you agree to:

- Post only real, accurate, and current listings, including the price, address, number of slots, amenities, description, and photos.
- Upload only photos that you own or have permission to use, and that show the actual property.
- Keep your availability up to date, and mark a dorm as Full when no slots remain.
- Place the map pin at the dorm's true location.
- Respond honestly to inquiries, and not collect payments or deposits through false claims.
- Follow all laws that apply to renting out property in the Philippines.

## 6. Listing Review and Moderation

To protect users from fake or misleading listings, new listings may be reviewed by an administrator before they appear to Seekers. A listing may stay hidden until it is approved. We may reject, hide, edit for safety, or remove any listing, review, or account at our discretion, especially if it appears fake, misleading, unsafe, or in violation of these Terms. We do not guarantee that every listing will be approved.

## 7. Reviews and Ratings

Reviews must be truthful and respectful. Do not post content that is false, defamatory, hateful, threatening, or that reveals someone's private information. We may remove reviews that break these rules. By posting a review, you allow us to display it in the App together with your name as shown on your account.

## 8. Prohibited Conduct

You agree not to:

- Post fake, prank, duplicate, or misleading listings.
- Impersonate another person or misrepresent your role or ownership of a property.

- Use the App for scams, spam, or any illegal activity.
- Collect other users' personal information without permission.
- Attempt to hack, disrupt, copy, or reverse-engineer the App or its services.
- Upload viruses, harmful code, or inappropriate, offensive, or unlawful content.

## 9. Safety Notice

We do not inspect properties, and we do not verify every statement made by landlords or reviewers. For your safety, we strongly advise Seekers to visit a dorm in person, ideally with a friend or family member, and to avoid sending money before seeing the property and confirming the landlord's identity. Contact between users happens through your phone's calling and messaging apps, and is outside our control.

# Part 2: Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction

Dorm the Explorer respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have. We handle personal data in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines.

## 2. Information We Collect

**Account information.** Your full name, email address, phone number, and chosen role (Seeker or Landlord). Your password is handled by our sign-in service and is not stored by us in readable form.

**Landlord listing information.** Dorm name, address, monthly price, slots, amenities, description, photos, and the map location (GPS coordinates) of the dorm. A landlord's name and contact details are shown with each listing.

**Activity information.** Favorites you save, and ratings and reviews you write.

**Location information.** If you allow location access, the App uses your device location to show directions and estimated distance to a dorm. You can turn this off in your phone's settings, and the App will still work, but these features may be limited.

**Technical information.** Basic device and usage data that our service providers collect to keep the App working and secure, such as app version and error logs.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account, and show you the right screens for your role.
- Display, search, and filter dorm listings, and show them on a map.
- Let Seekers contact Landlords by call or text.
- Show ratings, reviews, favorites, and availability.
- Review listings, prevent fake or abusive content, and keep the App safe.
- Fix problems and improve the App.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Consent

By creating an account and using the App, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described here. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us, but some features may stop working, and we may need to close your account.

## 5. Who Can See Your Information

- **Other users:** A landlord's name, phone number, and listing details are visible to Seekers so they can make contact. A reviewer's name and review are visible on the dorm's page. Your email is not shown to other users.
- **Service providers:** We use Firebase (Google) to run sign-in, the database, and photo storage, and Google Maps for map features. These providers process data on our behalf.
- **Administrators:** The App's administrator can view account and listing information in order to approve listings and handle reports.
- **Legal reasons:** We may disclose information if required by law or a valid legal request, or to protect the rights and safety of users.

## 6. Storage, Security, and Retention

Your data is stored with our cloud service providers, which may keep it on servers outside the Philippines. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it, such as secure sign-in and access controls. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password. We keep your information only as long as needed to run the service or as required by law. When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data, except where we must keep it for legal reasons.

## 7. Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

- Be informed about how your data is collected and used.
- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate or outdated information, which you can also do in your Profile.
- Object to certain processing, and withdraw your consent.
- Request that your data be blocked, removed, or deleted.
- Receive a copy of your data in a common format, where applicable.
- Be compensated for damages caused by inaccurate, false, or unlawfully obtained data.
- File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines if you believe your rights were violated.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below.
